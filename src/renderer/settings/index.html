Security

What Mi Browser is doing, right now, to keep your connection and your files safe.

Sandboxed browsing

Always on

Every tab runs in an isolated, sandboxed process — a malicious page can't reach the rest of your computer or your other tabs.

Encrypted DNS

—

Looks up site addresses over an encrypted connection, so your Wi-Fi network or ISP can't see which sites you're visiting just from DNS traffic.

Changing this needs a restart of Mi Browser to take effect.

HTTPS-Only Mode

Upgrades every site to a secure connection automatically. Pages that only work over plain HTTP (some older or local devices) will fail to load rather than being shown insecurely.

Risky download warnings

—

Pauses and warns on file types commonly used to spread malware before they land on disk. Manage this under Download safety.

Privacy & data

Control what Mi Browser blocks, and what it remembers.

Clear browsing data

Empties cache, cookies and other site data, and closes every open tab. Your saved settings are untouched.

Download safety

Mi Browser can flag risky downloads before they land on your disk. This is a heuristic check based on file type, not a real antivirus scan.

Set to 0 to switch this warning off entirely.
