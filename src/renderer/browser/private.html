What this tab actually forgets

No history. Close this tab and it's like it never happened, as far as Mi Browser is concerned.
No cookies kept. Everything this tab picks up is thrown out the moment it closes — nothing carries over to next time.
No passwords saved. Log in all you like — nothing gets written to the vault from here.

The serious bit: this only hides your tracks from this computer. Your employer, your school, your Wi-Fi network, your ISP and the websites you actually visit can still see exactly what you're up to. Private Browsing is a "not on this machine" promise, not an invisibility cloak — so act accordingly.
