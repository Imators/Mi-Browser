Why Mi Browser?

The name "Mi Browser" wasn't picked at random. "Mi" stands for "My Internet" – your internet. Founded in 2024 by Imators, Mi Browser embodies the vision of a web where everyone takes back ownership of their online experience, without compromise or surveillance.

Unlike traditional browsers, Mi Browser doesn't collect any personal data, doesn't track your browsing and doesn't show intrusive adverts. Every feature has been designed to put you at the centre of the experience, with a clean interface that doesn't distract from what matters.

Mi Browser includes a groundbreaking technology: an advanced protocol that misleads data brokers. This protection can't be blocked or detected, working quietly in the background to scramble your digital footprint and make your profile unusable to data collectors.

Our philosophy is simple: you should own your digital life. Mi Browser gives you the freedom to browse without surveillance, backed by powerful tools that actively protect your anonymity while keeping things fast and smooth.

Join a growing community of people who've chosen to take back control. Mi Browser is My Internet – browsing, reinvented on your own terms.
